# Jerusalem Lions Launch

Build a modern professional sports website for a new hockey club called Jerusalem HC.

The website is the official launch and founding membership crowdfunding page for the team.

The design should feel like a premium European hockey club website with a dark navy and gold color scheme, dramatic lighting, and professional sports visuals.

Primary colors:
Navy Blue (#0B1F3B)
Gold (#C8A44D)
White (#FFFFFF)

Typography should feel modern, strong, and athletic.

The site should be optimized for mobile first.

HERO SECTION

Full screen hero with arena lighting background.

Display:

Jerusalem HC logo (lion crest with Jerusalem wall crown).

Headline:

JERUSALEM HC
Israel’s First Professional Hockey Club

Subtext:

Launching the first full-time professional ice hockey team in Israel.

Call to action buttons:

• Become a Founding Member
• Learn About The Project

Show founding membership progress:

Goal: $300,000
Members Target: 500

Display a live progress bar.

THE VISION SECTION

Title:

Building the Future of Israeli Hockey

Text:

Jerusalem HC is being created to bring Israeli hockey to the next level.

For nearly 40 years, hockey has been growing in Israel through local leagues and national teams. Now, Jerusalem HC will become the first full-time professional hockey club based in Israel.

The club will compete against professional teams from Romania, Hungary, and across Europe while also playing in Israel’s top domestic league, the INHL.

The goal is to provide Israeli players the opportunity to develop at a high level while representing Israel internationally.

Jerusalem HC will host a minimum of 20 international home games each season.

Text:

The club identity is built around the Lion of Jerusalem, representing strength, resilience, and pride.
